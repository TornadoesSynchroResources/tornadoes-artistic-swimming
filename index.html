Dryland Flexibility Clothing

Athletes regularly complete flexibility and technical work on land. They must wear clothing that allows full and unrestricted movement.
• Required: Stretch shorts / elastic athletic shorts, leggings, or other flexible athletic clothing.
• Not permitted: Jeans or other rigid/restrictive fabrics that do not allow proper flexibility work.

Standardized Figure Attire

• Black swimsuit: Completely plain, with no brands, logos, names, letters, or other visible markings.
• White swim cap: Completely plain, with no brands, logos, names, letters, or other visible markings.
• Goggles and nose clip.
This standard maintains a clean, uniform and professional presentation during evaluation and competition.

Artistic Swimming Categories & Pathway Guide

Understanding local developmental tiers (Novice, Intermediate) vs. World Aquatics international elite divisions (12&U, Youth, Junior, Senior).

Development Tier 1

Novice (Principiante)

Focused on introduction to the sport and acquiring basic aquatic skills. Typically for swimmers in their first or second year of experience.
• Competitive Focus: Learning basic compulsory figures (fixed body positions and controlled transitions).
• Routines: Primarily team routines in local and recreational events.

Development Tier 2

Intermediate (Intermedio)

Serving as a bridge between recreational basics and high performance. For athletes with over 1 year of experience.
• Competitive Focus: Compulsory figures with higher technical complexity.
• Routines: Team, duet, and solo choreographies emphasizing synchronization, aerobic endurance, and smooth transitions.

World Aquatics International Elite Pathway (12&U, Youth, Junior, Senior)

12 & Under

Ages 12 & under. Foundation of international competition, featuring individual compulsory figures alongside free routines.

Youth (13–15)

Key junior transition category combining figure scores with high-energy free routines and acrobatic elements.

Junior (15–19)

High-performance feeder tier for national teams. Replaces figures with technical routines, free routines, and acrobatic routines.

Senior (Elite)

Peak international level (Olympic Games & World Championships) featuring technical, free, and acrobatic team/duet events.
