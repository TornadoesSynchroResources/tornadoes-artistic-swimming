Mandatory Athlete Gear & Equipment Matrix

Category-specific checklist for pool sessions, land conditioning, and national trials.

Category 1 & 2

Supernovices & Novices

  • Pool Wear: Plain black standard swimsuit & white swim cap.
  • Pool Gear: Clear goggles + 2 silicone nose clips (labeled).
  • Land Gear: Yoga mat, light resistance loop bands, black shorts/leggings.
  • Hydration: Insulated 1L reusable water bottle.
Category 3

Intermediate Squad

  • Pool Wear: Solid black training suit + Tornadoes official club cap.
  • Pool Gear: 2x nose clips, stationary sculling pads/buoys.
  • Conditioning: Yoga mat, medium resistance bands, 0.5kg ankle weights.
  • Hair Kit: Gelatin (Knox), hairpins, hairnet for trial rehearsals.
Category 4

12 & Under Squad

  • Pool Wear: Black high-neck training suits, white & club caps.
  • Pool Gear: Spare nose clips (min. 3), figure testing white cap.
  • Land Gear: High-density yoga mat, heavy resistance bands, jump rope.
  • Trial Gear: Plain black trial suit (no logos for judge anonymity).
Category 5

13–15 Youth Squad

  • Pool Gear: Standard black trial suits, club cap, competitive nose clips.
  • Land Tech: Flexibility stretching blocks, heavy resistance loops.
  • Routine Tools: Personal dryland count/music player.
  • Hair & Makeup: Full gelatin presentation kit & neutral gel set.
Category 6

Junior National Squad

  • Pool Wear: FINA/World Aquatics approved plain trial black suit + plain white cap.
  • Conditioning: 1.0kg ankle weights, stretch bands, core yoga mat.
  • Trials Kit: 4x backup nose clips, custom routine count sheet, timer.
  • Recovery: Foam roller, hydration electrolyte packs.

World Aquatics (WA) Official Competition Code & Rules

International scoring system, Coach Card declarations, hybrid difficulty families, and penalties.

Elements Panel (5 Judges)

Evaluates declared Technical Required Elements (TRE), Free Hybrids, and Acrobatics. Any under-rotation over 90° or missed declared element triggers an automatic Base Mark (BM) penalty.

Artistic Impression (5 Judges)

Evaluates Choreography, Music Interpretation, Manner of Presentation, and Transitions. Focuses on fluidity, expressiveness, and artistic execution between technical hybrids.

Technical Controllers (DTC & STC)

3 Difficulty Technical Controllers verify if declared movements match the Coach Card. 3 Synchronization Technical Controllers detect and log timing errors among athletes.

World Aquatics Key Penalties & Execution Limits

  • Bottom of Pool Penalty (-8.0 Pts): Deliberately pushing off or using pool floor during a hybrid or lift result in a massive deduction.
  • Deck Work Limit (10 Seconds): Deck movements before entering water cannot exceed 10 seconds. Overtime incurs a -2.0 penalty.
  • Base Mark (BM) Trigger: If an athlete performs lower difficulty than declared on the Coach Card, default minimum Base Mark DD is awarded.
  • Rotation Allowances: 360° rotations have a 90° tolerance; 180° rotations must be exact with no under-rotation allowance.

Aquatic Sports Association of Jamaica (ASAJ) Synchro Division

Jamaica's governing body for aquatic disciplines, national selection guidelines, and regional competition pathways.

ASAJ Jamaican National Selection Policy

  • Mandatory Trial Sessions: Candidates must maintain attendance in Wednesday (4:00-6:30 PM) & Saturday (2:00-5:00 PM) trial training sessions.
  • Single Attempt Standard: Trial figures and element evaluations strictly enforce single-attempt scoring to ensure high competitive discipline.
  • Passport Eligibility: Athletes representing Jamaica internationally (CCCAN, PANAM) must hold a valid Jamaican passport.

Regional Pathways & Meets

  • ASAJ Dev Meet & Trials (Nov 28): First official national selection benchmark.
  • CCCAN Championships: Central American and Caribbean regional competition pipeline.
  • Panam Aquatics Age Group: Elite international target for Youth and Junior squads.
